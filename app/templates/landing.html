Built to be careful, not just clever

Most symptom chatbots jump to an answer. This one is designed around the questions a careful person would ask first.

Consultation-style conversation

Describe the problem naturally. It remembers what you already said and never asks the same thing twice.

Every remedy has a source

Author, book and record number appear with each remedy. If the books have nothing relevant, it says so instead of guessing.

Safety checked separately

Warning signs are checked by fixed rules, not by the language model. Emergencies get an immediate message, never a home remedy.

Your language, automatically

Type or speak in English, Tamil, Telugu or Hindi. The reply follows your language, with a manual switch if needed.

Follow-up check-ins

After a day or two it asks how you are doing. If you are worse, it advises seeing a doctor instead of continuing a remedy.

General lifestyle education

Sleep, activity, hydration and eating information from public-health sources, never a personal diet plan.

How it works

  1. Tell it what is wrong

    Type or speak, in any of four languages, even mixed.

  2. It asks what is missing

    Duration, severity and warning signs, a few short questions at most.

  3. Rules check the risk

    Low, medium or high, decided by transparent safety rules.

  4. Books are searched

    Symptom-aware retrieval finds matching records and shows their source.

A knowledge base made from real books

The answers come from a curated dataset of 5,649 records collected from Ayurvedic books and government publications, in English and Tamil, with author, book and risk label preserved exactly as collected. The records were not written by a generative AI, and the app never modifies them.

The language model only explains what was retrieved. It cannot add remedies, doses or sources that are not in the records.

Retrieval
Multilingual embeddings and a FAISS index compare your symptoms with the symptoms documented in each record.
When the books are silent
A controlled fallback may show clearly labelled information from trusted health authorities, kept separate from the books.

Speak and listen in your language

Voice input uses Whisper speech recognition. Replies can be read aloud with your device's voices, when your device has one for your language.
